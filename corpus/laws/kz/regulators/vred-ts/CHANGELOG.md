## 2026-09-10 (date and time of the inspection act — `Instant` moments)

- Clause 1 of annex 2 requires «дату, время проведения и составления акта»
  [the date, time of conducting the inspection and of drafting the act];
  both fields stored only `Date`, and a module comment explained this by
  the absence of a moment type for case facts. The type exists: `Instant` is part of
  the v1 slice, allowed as a parameter of an empirical relation and asserted by a
  literal `@2026-08-30T10:00:00+05:00` (as `w3c/prov` does); outside the slice
  only `LocalTime` and `OffsetDateTime`. Parameters
  `akt_data_provedeniya_osmotra` and `akt_data_sostavleniya` were converted to
  `moment: Instant`, labels — "Date and time …". The group rule reads
  existence via `_` and did not change; the §57 moment comparison does not apply to `Date`,
  but the clause 3 deadlines run from the day the report was received, not from these
  fields — there is no comparison in any rule.
- Scenarios: 13 assertions in `tests/osmotr/09`, `tests/osmotr/19`, and
  `tests/regression/vred-ts.lawtest` gained a time of day and a UTC+5 offset.
  Families 73/33/65 pass on both evaluators, matrix 45 rows / 67 rules /
  169 tests with no issues, parity current, `lint.py` clean. CLIR and the two
  consumers (`ogpo-vts`, `uproshchennoe-uregulirovanie`) were rebuilt, the training-case
  lock was re-pinned, `check.py` 8/19.
- Form `apps/demos/akt-osmotra`: the `Instant`-typed field — `datetime-local` with
  the offset next to it (hint `+05:00`, editable); the engine receives the value
  `urn:law:std#Instant`, and the `.lawcase` text gets the literal `@…`.
- Not done, and noted: the `ogpo-vts` lock pinned the `vred_ts` CLIR hash, which did not
  match HEAD even before this change and was not updated; the registry index
  `apps/registry/index` at HEAD lags CLIR by about thirty packages; its
  rebuild is not part of this change.
