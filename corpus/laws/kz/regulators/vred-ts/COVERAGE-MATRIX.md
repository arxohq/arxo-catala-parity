## Open questions for the owner

- **Q1.** Clause 3-1 lifts the «сроки осуществления страховщиком осмотра и составления акта осмотра, определения размера вреда, указанные в пункте 3» [the time limits for the insurer's conduct of the inspection and drawing up of the inspection report, and for determining the amount of damage, referred to in clause 3]. Does this extend to the time limit for the INJURED PARTY'S MARK (three business days from the day the report is received), which is not a time limit "for the insurer's conduct" of anything? Reading adopted pending a decision: all three time limits of clause 3 are lifted (mark, reply, report); tests KZ-VRED-20, t11-d, S09.
- **Q2.** The second paragraph of clause 9 gives two payout alternatives ("transfers … the remains … or makes … a payout net of"), without naming who chooses the alternative. Reading adopted pending a decision: the alternative is determined by the established fact of the transfer of the remains, or by its established negation; case silence means the payout is undetermined (§113).
- **Q3.** Clause 9 does not name a lower bound for the payout "net of the value of the remains fit for sale": when the value of the remains exceeds the market value, the arithmetic yields a negative amount. Reading adopted pending a decision: the formula is applied literally (case G14), zero is not implied as a floor — pending the owner's decision.
